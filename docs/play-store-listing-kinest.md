## App name

Kinest

## Short description

Keep your family connected: live location, SOS alerts, chat and calls.

## Full description

Kinest keeps your family connected, located and safe, in one private app that only your family can see.

LIVE FAMILY MAP
• See where each family member is, if they choose to share their location
• Follow someone on the map as they move, and see how far away they are
• Timeline: look back over the last 24 hours or 7 days
• Save places such as Home or Office and get a note when someone arrives or leaves
• Weather, battery level, network signal and last-seen time on every family card

SOS ALERTS
• One tap sends an SOS with your live location to every family you belong to
• A loud siren and a full-screen alert on their phones, even if the app is closed or the phone is locked
• Optional voice clip and photo, a Quick Settings tile, and shake to send
• A 3-second countdown with Cancel, and "I'm safe now" to tell everyone you are fine
• Optional Nearby Help: nearby Kinest users who opted in can be asked to phone the right emergency number for you. They never see your name or number

CHAT AND CALLS
• Family group chat and private chats, with photos, videos, voice messages and files
• Reply to a message straight from its notification, without opening the app
• Voice and video calls inside the app, with up to 6 people in one call
• Calls are never recorded

MORE SAFETY TOOLS
• Find My Phone: ring a misplaced phone, even on silent
• Phone lost mode: track and ring a lost phone closely
• Fake call: a pretend incoming call to help you leave a situation
• Offline SMS alerts: with no internet, your last known position is texted to your family
• Optional overspeed alert and crash detection

YOUR PRIVACY
• Your data belongs to you and your family. It is not sold and not used for advertising
• You decide what your family can see: location, online status and last seen can each be switched off
• Location history is deleted after 7 days, and messages after 90 days
• Delete your account and your data at any time, in the app or on the website

AVAILABLE IN
English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

Kinest helps your family stay in touch, but it does not replace the emergency services. In an emergency, call 112.

Kinest uses your location in the background so your family can see where you are and so SOS works when the app is closed. You can turn location sharing off at any time in Profile, Privacy.
